Shipping Information

Standard Shipping

Delivery within 5-7 business days. Free on orders over $100.

Express Shipping

Delivery within 2-3 business days. Additional charges apply.

Overnight Shipping

Next business day delivery. Order before 2 PM for overnight processing.

Order Processing

Orders are processed within 1-2 business days. You'll receive a confirmation email once your order ships.

Returns & Exchanges

Return Policy

Items can be returned within 30 days of purchase. Products must be unworn, unwashed, and in original condition with all tags attached.

How to Return

1. Log into your account and go to Order History
2. Select the item(s) you want to return
3. Print the prepaid return label
4. Pack the item securely and ship it back

Refund Processing

Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days after we receive your return. The refund will be issued to your original payment method.

Exchanges

For exchanges, please return the original item and place a new order. This ensures you receive your replacement item quickly.

Orders

Order Confirmation

You'll receive an order confirmation email immediately after placing your order. This email contains your order number and details.

Order Changes

To modify or cancel an order, contact customer service within 24 hours of placing the order. After this period, changes may not be possible.

Out of Stock Items

If an item becomes unavailable after you order, we'll notify you immediately and issue a refund for that item.

Payments

Accepted Payment Methods

We accept Visa, MasterCard, American Express, Discover, PayPal, Apple Pay, and Google Pay.

Payment Security

All transactions are secured with SSL encryption. We never store your full credit card information.

Billing Issues

If you experience any billing issues, please contact customer service with your order number for assistance.

Account Help

Creating an Account

Click on "Sign Up" and provide your email address and a password. You can also sign up using social media accounts.

Password Reset

Click "Forgot Password" on the login page and enter your email. We'll send you a link to reset your password.

Account Settings

Log into your account to update your profile, change your password, manage addresses, and view order history.

Delivery Information

Delivery Times

Standard: 5-7 business days | Express: 2-3 business days | Overnight: Next business day

Delivery Issues

If your package is delayed or damaged, contact customer service immediately with your tracking number.

Signature Required

Some orders may require a signature upon delivery. If you're not available, the carrier will leave a notice for pickup.

Our customer service team is here to assist you.
